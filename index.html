01 / THE RISK

A site can look fine and still shut people out.

Keyboard traps and missing form labels are easy to miss in a routine site review. So is weak contrast. A complaint makes those problems much harder to ignore.

3,117 federal lawsuits in 2025

Seyfarth Shaw counted 3,117 federal website accessibility lawsuits in 2025, up 27% from 2024. Read the docket review.

That count is only part of the picture

The same review says its total does not include state-court cases or demand letters. It also notes that some federal cases may not appear in keyword searches.

A scanner does not settle the question

Automated checks are useful, but they cannot judge every real user task. Keyboard testing and human review still matter.

Common barriers are easy to overlook

Missing labels, poor keyboard support, weak contrast, and inaccessible forms can sit behind an otherwise polished design.

What does website accessibility mean?

People should be able to use the site with a keyboard, screen reader, captions, magnification, or other assistive technology. WCAG is the technical standard most teams use to measure that work.

Can a website lead to a lawsuit?

Yes. U.S. businesses have faced lawsuits and demand letters over barriers that allegedly deny equal access. The law varies by jurisdiction and by the facts, so ask qualified counsel about your own exposure.

What does the $249 audit cover?

We agree on the scope first, then review key pages and user paths. The report covers issues involving keyboard use, structure, forms, images, contrast, links, and interactive controls.

Do you fix the issues?

We can quote Fix work for WordPress and Shopify sites, plus custom-coded sites, when we can access the relevant code. The quote is separate from the $249 audit.

What if my site uses a closed builder?

That includes Wix and Squarespace sites, plus GoDaddy Website Builder. We can start with Alert and Audit, then Monitor. We do not offer Fix work on closed builders, but the report will explain what needs attention.

What does monitoring catch?

Monitoring checks for new, detectable issues as the site changes. It gives you an early warning. It cannot find every barrier or remove every legal risk, so human review still matters.
