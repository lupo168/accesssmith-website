What does website accessibility mean?

People should be able to use the site with a keyboard, screen reader, captions, magnification, or other assistive technology. WCAG is the technical standard most teams use to measure that work.

Can a website lead to a lawsuit?

Yes. U.S. businesses have faced lawsuits and demand letters over barriers that allegedly deny equal access. The law varies by jurisdiction and by the facts, so ask qualified counsel about your own exposure.

What does the USD 249 accessibility audit cover?

We agree on the scope first, then review key pages and user paths. The report covers issues involving keyboard use, structure, forms, images, contrast, links, and interactive controls.

What is directory listing management?

We submit and maintain your business listings across major online directories, keeping your name, address, phone, hours, and categories consistent and accurate — so customers find the right information wherever they look.

What's the difference between the directory tiers?

Basic (USD 49) covers 10 core directories with a one-time setup. Standard (USD 129) expands to 20 directories with category optimization and photo/hours verification. Managed (USD 229/year) adds ongoing monitoring, duplicate suppression, and monthly reports.

What is a GEO audit?

GEO (generative engine optimization) measures how your business shows up when customers ask AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini for recommendations. Our audit covers two parts: AI Exposure (does the AI know and mention your brand?) and Citation Rate (does it cite your website as a source?), plus a competitor appendix. You get a diagnosis and a prioritized fix list for each part.

What's the difference between the audit, the fix implementation, and monitoring?

Three separate services. The audit (USD 499) diagnoses both parts and gives you the fix list. Fix implementation (USD 999) is us executing that list for you. Monitoring (USD 49/mo) re-checks monthly and alerts you when AI answers shift. Buy them together or separately.

What does the AI Overviews coverage check do?

We search your brand terms and record whose pages the AI overview cites — yours, a review site, or a competitor. If a competitor is intercepting clicks on searches for your own brand, the report flags it and recommends fixes.

Can you guarantee AI citations or rankings?

No — and be wary of anyone who does. We audit readiness and give you a prioritized roadmap. Whether an AI assistant cites you depends on many factors outside anyone's control.

Do you offer phone calls or meetings?

No. Everything runs over email at hi@accesssmith.com. It's faster, it's documented, and it keeps our prices where they are.
